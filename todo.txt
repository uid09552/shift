1) what features to add additionally, print here

   UI ideas are already collected in docs/ui-proposals.md (hours account, scenario
   comparison, coverage forecast, fairness dashboard, absence requests, swaps,
   publish/notify, ...). Start there with #3 "coverage forecast": it would have
   shown before planning that nobody can work "Rufdienst" (see note under 2).

   Not in that doc, ordered by value for a hospital roster:

   a) Plan across period boundaries. Every solve starts from nothing: a night on
      the 31st followed by an early shift on the 1st, or 6 days in a row ending
      on the 31st plus 3 more from the 1st, are not seen. Feed the last ~7 days
      of the confirmed roster into the solver as fixed history (today
      locked_assignments outside the period are dropped as unknown dates).
   b) Skill mix per shift, not only per workstation: "at least one person with
      Schichtleitung / Praxisanleitung on every shift", "max 1 trainee per
      shift". Today a workstation requires every capability from every person.
   c) Weekend rules: at least N free weekends per month, and "both days or
      neither" (no lone Saturday). Standard in German hospital agreements.
   d) Date-specific demand: public holidays (per Bundesland) and special days
      override the per-weekday min/max; holidays also count for hours accounts.
   e) Minimum-staffing mode per workstation (hard for ICU/OR, soft elsewhere)
      instead of one global switch.
   f) Sick-call replacement: when someone calls in sick on a confirmed day,
      list who could legally take the slot (rest, max days, skills), ranked by
      fairness and hours balance, and assign in one click. Minimal-change
      re-plan of the following days as an option.
   g) Closures, next steps: a reason per closure (renovation, maintenance,
      holiday), recurring closures (e.g. Tagesklinik every weekend), and a
      warning listing confirmed assignments that fall inside a new closure.
   h) Departments inside a tenant: planners scoped to their wards, so the
      Station A planner cannot change the ICU roster.
   i) Payroll export: actual hours per person with night/Sunday/holiday
      surcharges (Zuschläge), as CSV/DATEV.
   j) Webhooks on plan confirmed / closure created / wish decided, so the n8n
      instance that is already running can send notifications and sync.

2) optimize the planner algorithm, its still not planning all shifts, it must distribute the people to min. amount of people per workstation and shift
   DONE. The solver now fills minimum staffing first (phase 1), then optimises
   balance, wishes, fatigue etc. without giving back a filled slot (phase 2).
   Before, hour balancing could outweigh a slot (long shifts, part-timers), so it
   left slots empty. Slots still short are listed in the result's message, with
   the reason. Tests: planner/tests/test_staffing.py (make test in planner/).
   Note on the current data: the weekend "Rufdienst" at Notaufnahme can never be
   filled. Only the ICU nurses have it among their available shifts, and they
   lack the Notaufnahme/Wundversorgung skills. That is a setup gap, not a solver
   problem.

3) implement deactivation of workstations, from-to, or complete
   DONE. Workstations page: "Deactivate" per row (and in the right-click menu)
   opens a dialog, "for a period" or "completely"; the Status column shows
   Active / Closed until ... / Deactivated, plus a planned closure. The weekly
   schedule (by workstation / by shift) and the dashboard no longer count a
   closed workstation as understaffed. New API: GET /workstation-unavailabilities;
   overlapping closures are refused.
   Both kinds of closure (period / complete) are honoured everywhere: solver,
   its output check, agent validate/fix, hand edits in the schedule and employee
   calendar (closed stations cannot be picked; the API refuses them too), the
   scheduler's proposal editor, and "Take as Plan" (refuses a proposal that
   still staffs a closed station).
