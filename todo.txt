Planner / optimizer

11) Period boundaries — the solver sees only the planning period, so a night on the
    31st doesn't block the 1st and rest/consecutive-day limits restart every month.
    Send the last N days of the confirmed roster as read-only history. [M]
    DONE. The backend sends the confirmed roster of the 14 days before the period
    as `history` (TaskDTO, /planner/prepare and /planner/plan). The solver carries
    recovery days, minimum rest and running streaks (max_consecutive_days) across
    the start; blocked slots get no variable, so locks/rotations on them are
    named in `message`, never infeasible. The agent's plan check follows the same
    history. Not carried: the weekly day cap (7-day blocks from the period start).
    Tests: planner/tests/test_history.py.

14) Personal limits — per employee max nights per month, max weekends, no nights
    (e.g. pregnancy, age), preferred days off. Soft or hard, like min_staffing_mode. [M]
    DONE. Employees → edit → "Personal limits": max night shifts and max weekends
    per calendar month, "no night shifts", preferred weekdays off (table
    employee_personal_limits, migration 29; GET/PUT /employees/{id}/personal-limits).
    Planner settings → "Personal limits": rule (hard, default) or target (soft —
    exceeded only to fill a shift that would stay short, as little as possible,
    and named in the result). No night shifts is always hard; preferred days off
    cost preference_weight like one-off preferred days. The assistant's plan check
    flags all four. Tests: planner/tests/test_personal_limits.py,
    tests/personal_limits.rs.

16) Short-notice replacement — "Anna is sick tomorrow": rank qualified, rested, under-
    hours colleagues for that slot, using the repair logic for one slot only. [M]
    DONE. Schedule page → a shift's menu → "Find replacement…": everyone the rules
    allow (repair's blocking_reason — skills, rest, recovery, streaks, weekly cap,
    station maximum, hard personal limits) ranked by wish, preferred day off,
    hours below the month's target, then rest; everyone else with the reason.
    Shows how many are left on the shift against its minimum. "Assign" marks the
    absent person sick / vacation / absent and puts the colleague on the shift.
    Agent endpoint POST /agent/roster/replacements (read-only). The repair now
    also respects personal limits. Tests: agent/tests/test_replacement.py
    (make test in agent/).
